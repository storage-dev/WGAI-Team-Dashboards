Method & limits

Generated by tools/social_media_audit.py in snapshot mode from the Blotato publishing record for every connected account, covering 02 Jun 2026 – 31 Aug 2026 (919 records: 894 published, 25 failed).

Reach data is only as complete as the platforms allow. 7 of 894 posts returned metrics. Any platform absent from the measured set reports nothing back through the current plan, so no claim is made about its actual reach — the absence of that data is itself a finding.

Brand attribution is exact where the platform exposes the account — handles in post URLs and page IDs in Facebook URLs. Where the URL carries no account identifier, attribution falls back to caption vocabulary: 162 posts were attributed that way and 34 could not be classified at all. Per-brand splits on those platforms are close estimates, not exact counts.

Thresholds that decide severity live in social_audit_config.json and can be tuned per organisation without touching the tool.
